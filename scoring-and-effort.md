# Project Revival — Scoring and Calibration

Round 1 base criteria: technical depth 25, code originality 20, system architecture 15, execution quality 15, design and creativity 15, growth potential 10. Scores must be whole numbers.

Optional SDG bonus: +0 to +2, outside base score, for SDGs 4, 8, 9, 11, 12, 17. No claim = +0 and no penalty. Total ranking is out of 102, descending by total. Ties follow the event-rules tie-break order.

Before full screening, score 3–5 sample submissions, review disagreements with organizers, freeze the rule version and anchors, then score the full batch. Unscorable private/unreachable/unverified-environment submissions go to a clarification queue during the published cure period rather than automatically ranking last.

Round 2 uses one cohort-wide tier, selected in event rules:
- Tier A: 1–2 points, focused integration.
- Tier B: 2–3 points, bounded end-to-end; only with at least 4 active coding hours and low dependency risk.
- Tier C disabled unless explicitly enabled.
