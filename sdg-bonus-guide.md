# Optional SDG Bonus — Project Revival

SDG alignment is optional and never an eligibility requirement. Event focus goals:
- SDG 4 — Quality Education
- SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth
- SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure
- SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities
- SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production
- SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals

Bonus, outside base 100:
- +0: no claim or no credible causal connection; no penalty.
- +1: plausible, specific connection between actual functionality and a named focus SDG.
- +2: clear, supported connection with appropriately qualified impact claims.

Rank by total /102. If totals tie, apply the published tie-break order. SDG labels/icons alone are not evidence. Do not force SDG features into Round 2.
